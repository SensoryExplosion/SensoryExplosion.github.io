Customers had to process the product before they could value it.

The audit showed that the experience made protection feel abstract, effortful, and harder to trust.

01

Value arrived too late

Features, policy details, and conditions appeared before customers understood why protection mattered.

Lead with relatable risk, care, and reassurance - then introduce price and coverage.

02

Complexity invited drop-off

Dense formal copy, weak chunking, and long interactions created cognitive load, especially during purchase.

Use progressive disclosure, focused steps, and simpler consent.

03

Trust lacked consistency

Tone shifted between friendly and formal, while the hierarchy underplayed important information.

Use a human voice, native patterns, clearer emphasis, and social proof.
